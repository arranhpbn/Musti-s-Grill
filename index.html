Our Story

From a Grill Van to Your High Street Table

Musti's Grill & Bistro started small: a family with a mobile grill van and a big love for authentic Turkish flavours. Word spread fast, and today we've grown into a warm, welcoming bistro right here on Horley High Street, serving the same recipes that started it all: flame-grilled kebabs, fresh wraps, hearty breakfasts and homemade cakes.

Every plate is still cooked to order by our family, for yours. Pop in for a Turkish coffee, a full breakfast, or a mixed grill shared with friends. You're always welcome at our table.

Hosting a birthday or private function? Book the restaurant, or bring the grill to you with our mobile van. We're fully licensed to serve alcohol, including wine, beer and spirits. Ask us for details.
